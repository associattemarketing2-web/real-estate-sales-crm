# Design System

## UX principles
- Action-oriented rather than report-heavy.
- Salesperson home asks: “What should I do today?”
- Keep Lead 360 accessible from every lead reference.
- Make status, owner, next action and next-action date obvious.
- Use progressive disclosure for complex property/inventory data.
- Optimize common actions for desktop while remaining responsive.
- Avoid visual clutter and unnecessary CRM fields.

## Navigation
Dashboard, Leads, Opportunities, Projects, Inventory, Site Visits, Follow-ups, Communications, Campaigns, Reports, Settings.

## Key reusable UI
Lead card, requirement summary, qualification panel, property-match card, inventory table, activity timeline, follow-up task, visit card, pipeline board, KPI card, filters/search and audit drawer.
