# Product Vision

## Positioning
AI-Powered Real Estate Sales CRM built around the actual property-sales lifecycle rather than a generic contact database.

## Primary outcome
Help sales teams convert more qualified opportunities by making every lead actionable: understand requirements, identify matching inventory, schedule the next activity and maintain follow-up discipline.

## Core workflow
Lead → Qualification → Project/Inventory Matching → Site Visit → Negotiation → Booking → Agreement → Closure

## Product questions
1. Who is this lead?
2. What do they want?
3. How qualified are they?
4. Which available properties fit?
5. What should happen next?

## Target users
Organization admins, sales managers, sales executives, telecallers, marketing users and approved external/channel partners.

## Differentiators
- Real-estate-native qualification fields.
- Project and unit-level inventory matching.
- Explainable lead scoring.
- Lead 360 timeline.
- Site-visit and follow-up discipline.
- Campaign-to-booking attribution.
- AI qualification, matching and next-best-action layers.

## MVP boundary
MVP focuses on organizations, users/roles, leads, requirements, activities, projects, inventory, matching, follow-ups, site visits and pipeline. Advanced AI and broad integrations follow after the core data model is stable.
