# Qualification Engine

Qualification must be transparent and explainable.

## Requirement fit example
- Budget fit: 20
- Location fit: 20
- BHK fit: 15
- Timeline fit: 15
- Loan readiness: 10
- Responded to outreach: 5
- Brochure/details engagement: 5
- Site visit: 10

Total: 100.

Suggested bands: 80–100 high, 60–79 medium, below 60 low. These are configurable business defaults, not permanent truths.

## Behavioral signals
Answered call, WhatsApp reply, brochure request, price/floorplan/payment-plan questions, site visit, revisit, negotiation and booking activity.

## AI role
AI can extract requirements from conversations, summarize objections and propose a score explanation. Deterministic scoring remains the auditable source for the actual score until a deliberate product decision changes it.
