# Modules and Acceptance Criteria

## Lead Management
Create/edit/search/filter leads, assign owner, detect likely duplicates, capture source and requirements, maintain status and next action.

## Lead 360
A single chronological view of profile, source, requirements, score, matched properties, communications, activities, visits, follow-ups and booking state.

## Projects & Inventory
Create projects, developers, configurations and units. Track availability, pricing and freshness. Support filters by location, BHK, budget and possession.

## Qualification
Calculate explainable score, show contributing factors and preserve score history.

## Property Match
Return matching projects/units with fit reasons, allow shortlist/share and record what was shown.

## Follow-ups & Visits
Create tasks, due dates, reminders, visit schedules and outcomes. Show overdue items.

## Dashboards
Salesperson: today’s actions. Manager: team funnel and compliance. Marketing: spend/leads/qualified/visits/bookings. Project: demand and inventory movement.

## Global acceptance rules
All modules must enforce role permissions, tenant isolation, validation, empty/loading/error states, audit behavior and responsive UI.
