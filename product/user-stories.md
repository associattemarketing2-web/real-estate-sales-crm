# Core User Stories

## Lead management
- As a salesperson, I can create/search/filter a lead and see the next action.
- As a manager, I can assign/reassign leads within my team.
- As a user, I can flag a likely duplicate before creating a second record.

## Qualification
- As a salesperson, I can see an explainable qualification score and factors.
- As a manager, I can see score history and changes over time.
- As a user, I can correct extracted requirements without losing the source interaction.

## Property matching
- As a salesperson, I can enter or edit requirements and receive ranked project/inventory matches.
- Each match shows reasons, gaps and current inventory/pricing freshness.
- I can create a shortlist and record what was shared.

## Follow-up and visits
- I can schedule a follow-up with a due time and owner.
- I can schedule a site visit and record outcome/no-show/reschedule.
- The dashboard highlights overdue actions.

## Sales
- I can move an opportunity through defined stages.
- I can record negotiation details without losing history.
- I can reserve/book inventory only when server-side business rules pass.

## Reporting
- Salesperson sees today's actions.
- Manager sees funnel, overdue followups and team activity.
- Marketing sees source/campaign performance through qualified leads, visits and bookings.
- Project view shows demand and inventory movement.
