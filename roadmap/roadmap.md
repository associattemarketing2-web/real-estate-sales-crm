# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Repository, product specification, architecture, database model, RLS/security model, roles, UX principles, testing strategy and acceptance criteria.

## Phase 1 — MVP CRM
Authentication, organizations, users/roles, lead management, Lead 360, requirements, projects, developers, configurations, inventory, pipeline, activities, follow-ups and site visits.

## Phase 2 — Automation
Meta lead ingestion, UTM/campaign attribution, WhatsApp/email integrations, notifications, webhook idempotency and automation monitoring through n8n.

## Phase 3 — Intelligence
Conversation extraction, qualification assistance, property matching assistance, next-best-action and manager insights with explainable outputs and human controls.

## Phase 4 — Platform
Advanced analytics, partner access, configurable workflows, integrations marketplace, higher-scale infrastructure and enterprise controls.

## Release gate
Do not move to the next phase until the current phase passes functional, permission, tenant-isolation, data-integrity and regression tests.
